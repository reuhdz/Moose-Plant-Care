Track per-plant tasks (repot, fertilize, prune, propagate, pest check, …) and general gardening reminders. Todos are saved on this device and included in the Export JSON backup.

🤖 Ask Claude

Chat with Claude (Anthropic's AI) about your plants. Your API key is stored only on this device — see Settings below. Each plant's stats are sent as context so Claude can answer about your collection, not just generic advice.

🔑 Set up your Anthropic API key

You'll need a key from console.anthropic.com (free signup, pay-as-you-go billing — typical chat is well under $0.01 per turn).

💾 Save to plant notes

Saved notes live in the Care Guide under 📝 Notes from past chats for the selected plant.

🪴 Current Soil Mix

Each card shows the soil mix you currently have your plant in (or pending if not set yet) plus a research-based recommendation. Update a plant's soil from the Log & Data → Plant Profile form.

📍 Plant Placement Guide

Where to put each plant in your specific home, based on the room conditions you've described. Each plant has an Ideal spot (will thrive) and an OK spot (will survive and look fine, just not at peak). Switch between the Rooms view (zones first, plants per zone) and the Plants view (plants first, zones per plant).
