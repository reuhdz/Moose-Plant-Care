Chat with Claude (Anthropic's AI) about your plants. Your API key is stored only on this device — see Settings below. Each plant's stats are sent as context so Claude can answer about your collection, not just generic advice.

🪴 Current Soil Mix

Each card shows the soil mix you currently have your plant in (or pending if not set yet) plus a research-based recommendation. Update a plant's soil from the Log & Data → Plant Profile form.

📍 Plant Placement Guide

Where each plant should live in your actual home — 8 windows across 2 floors in Pflugerville, TX (~30.4°N), matched by light + humidity. Each window lists what will thrive, what's also solid, and what to keep out. Use Focus on a plant to see exactly where one plant should go, or Focus on a room to narrow the view to a single window.
